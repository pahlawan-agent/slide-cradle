Geographical Access Barrier

Millions of Muslim youth in diaspora hubs and western municipalities have no access to qualified local scholars. The shortage of certified instructors for Hifz and Ijazah is severe and growing.

The Ed-Tech Gap

Casual platforms like Duolingo are built for conversational language — not rigorous Tajweed, multi-year Hifz, or Ijazah certification. The $44B online language market completely ignores this demographic.

Fragmented Alternatives

Students are left with unverified private tutors, outdated static websites, and basic video calls — no credential verification, no curriculum tracking, no AI diagnostics, no structured learning path.

A High-Intent, High-LTV Market Ignored

Hifz takes 2–4 years. Ijazah demands ongoing mentorship. This is a subscription-ready, high-lifetime-value market — driven by a 15% CAGR in global online tutoring and strong willingness to pay for premium personalized education.

1

Phoneme-Level Analysis

Breaks down recitations into individual phonemes, comparing against canonical Tajweed standards. Identifies Makhraj errors, Madd elongation, and Sifat characteristics.

2

Real-Time Alignment

Uses dynamic time warping and sequence-to-sequence models to align spoken words with Quranic text in real time, enabling word-by-word highlighting and error flagging.

3

Offline-First Architecture

All ML models run locally on device hardware (CPU/GPU). Zero cloud dependency ensures data privacy — no audio ever leaves the device. Critical for regions with poor connectivity.

4

Multi-Reciter Training Data

Trained on diverse recitation styles from world-renowned Qaris. Robust across different speeds, tones, and dialectal variations.
