Please list out all the technical modules/section/parts for your product or service that will be developed during CIP funding period (up to 18 months). *

Smart Onboarding & Identity Verification Module
This module establishes a frictionless entry point with distinct, role-based workflows for both Students and Experts. Beyond standard profile management for credentials and certifications, the core innovation here is the Automated Expert Verification sub-module. This system integrates ASR technology to perform an immediate, automated "pass/fail" analysis of an expert applicant's recitation quality. This acts as a primary filter to ensure only qualified Huffaz or Qaris enter the supply pool before undergoing manual review.
Proprietary ASR & Proficiency Analysis Engine
Serving as the application's core diagnostic intelligence, this engine powers the automated feedback features. It utilizes a Proficiency Classification Algorithm to listen to user input and instantly categorize their skill level as Beginner, Intermediate, or Advanced. This is paired with granular Error Detection Logic, which identifies specific Tajweed mistakes (such as Makhraj or Harakat errors) to provide immediate corrective feedback to the student and tag the query with accurate metadata for the experts.
Asynchronous Query & Intelligent Matchmaking Module
This module contains the platform's central logic for managing the interaction between students and experts. It begins with an Audio Query System that handles the recording, compression, and uploading of student inquiries. The routing is handled by a Broadcasting Algorithm that displays these queries to relevant experts based on the ASR-detected proficiency level. To ensure speed and engagement, the system utilizes Concurrency Control (a "Fastest Finger" logic) that strictly caps responses to three experts per query. The cycle concludes with a Selection & Feedback Loop, allowing the student to review the three responses and select the best one to trigger the reward.
Live Tutoring & Video Conferencing Module
Designed for users who wish to upgrade from simple Q&A to structured learning, this module facilitates a synchronous environment. It relies on seamless Video Integration (using protocols like WebRTC) to provide stable, low-latency video conferencing within the app. Supporting this is a complex Scheduling System that manages booking slots, expert availability, and time zone conversions. Additionally, Session Management logic tracks class duration and attendance to strictly validate that the service was delivered before payment is processed.
Financial Ledger & Multi-Split Payment Gateway
This backend system manages the entire flow of funds across the marketplace. It integrates a secure Payment Gateway to handle incoming transactions via FPX, credit cards, or e-wallets. The financial architecture is governed by a Commission Logic Engine, which automatically calculates and splits the platform’s take-rate versus the expert’s net payout. Crucially, it includes an Escrow & Payout System that holds student funds securely and only releases them to the expert’s digital wallet once a specific trigger (such as selecting a Q&A answer or completing a live class) is verified.
